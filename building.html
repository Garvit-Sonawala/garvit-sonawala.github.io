MindSort

Voice & text personal-assistant PWA

MindSort is a Progressive Web App I designed and built end to end. You "dump" whatever's on your mind, typed or spoken, in English, Hindi, or a mix of both, and it works out what you actually meant: a new task, something you've finished, a reason to reshape today's plan, or just you thinking out loud. Nothing gets written until you confirm it. Every morning it puts together a short, reasoned daily briefing from your real open tasks and behavioural signals it's picked up over time, things like which projects have gone quiet or which ones keep losing their slot, instead of just sorting everything by due date.

Under the hood it's a React PWA on Supabase Postgres, with row-level security on every table, voice transcription through Deepgram, and both AI providers called through server-side proxy functions so no API key ever reaches the browser. The model proposes changes, but a deterministic layer in the code re-verifies anything that actually matters, so it can't silently invent a due date or a finished task.
