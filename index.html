I am a fifth-year BS-MS student in Physics at IISER Thiruvananthapuram, while also pursuing a BS in Data Science from IIT Madras.

I have worked on problems ranging from dark matter freeze-out in the early Universe and gravitational-wave searches using pulsar timing arrays to rare B-meson decays and precision tests of the Standard Model. Across these projects, I start from the underlying theory, study how the system behaves, and build the computational tools needed to turn that understanding into quantitative predictions.

Outside research, I build software. This year I built and published MindSort, an app for organizing work that grew out of a simple question: task lists are everywhere, but why don't they tell you what actually deserves your attention? I like building tools that solve problems I keep running into myself, especially when a bit of automation can save time and reduce mental overhead.

Outside of work, I run regularly, you can follow that on Strava.
