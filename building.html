About Research Building

I like automating things that are repetitive and don't really need a human's judgement, usually with some machine learning or computation doing the actual thinking. Model a system well enough and you don't have to do the boring part by hand anymore.

MindSort

Voice & text personal-assistant PWA

MindSort is a Progressive Web App I designed and built end to end. You dump whatever's on your mind, typed or spoken, and it works out what you meant, a new task, something finished, or a reason to reshape today's plan, then sorts it into the right project on its own. Every morning it also acts like a personal assistant, suggesting what you should actually be working on, based on the patterns it's picked up over time rather than just due dates.
